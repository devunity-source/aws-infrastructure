Focus is a resource and noise is the tax on it. Good noise cancelling does not make a room silent, but it takes the HVAC, the traffic and the colleague on a call three desks over and turns them into a soft hush you stop noticing. These five have been through client offices, flights and one memorable kitchen renovation on our team.

## What we judged

**Noise cancelling on voices.** Low rumble is easy. Human speech is hard, and it is the thing that breaks concentration. We tested in a shared office with a podcast playing nearby.

**Microphone quality.** You will take calls on these. Half the market still sounds like a bathroom. The picks here do not.

**Comfort at hour four.** Clamp force, weight and ear-cup depth. Several great-sounding headphones fail here.

**Multipoint.** Connected to a laptop and a phone at the same time, so a call on either one just works.

Sony's sixth-generation flagship fixed the two complaints about the fifth: it folds again, and the call microphones are now as good as the noise cancelling. Twelve mics and a new processor make it the best at suppressing voices of anything we have worn. It stays comfortable through a working day.

The default sound is warm with a bass tilt. Spend two minutes in the app EQ and it is excellent. Touch controls on the cup work well indoors and poorly with gloves. It costs what a flagship costs.

<ProductCard index={1} {...frontmatter.products[1]} />

Bose has the lightest clamp of any premium over-ear, and for people who get a headache from headphones by lunch, that alone decides it. The second generation added lossless audio over USB-C and tightened the adaptive ANC to within a hair of Sony. The buttons are real buttons, which we appreciate.

Battery is good but not Sony good. The case is big. The app wants your attention more than it should.

Earbuds used to be the compromise option for noise cancelling. The third-generation AirPods Pro are not a compromise. The new foam-infused tips seal better, and the ANC is close enough to the over-ears that several of us wear them on flights now. The hearing-health features are genuinely useful. Pairing across a Mac, iPad and iPhone is instant.

On Android, you lose most of the clever features and they become good, expensive earbuds. Battery per charge is a few hours, so the case lives in your pocket.

<ProductCard index={3} {...frontmatter.products[3]} />

AirPods Max are heavy, the case is a joke, and the battery is the shortest here. We still have three pairs on the team. The reason is how they fit into a Mac workflow: they appear on the laptop before you have sat down, transparency mode sounds like not wearing headphones, and the aluminium and mesh build makes everything else feel like plastic.

The USB-C revision added lossless and low-latency audio over the cable. If you edit video or audio on a Mac, that matters. If you are on Windows or Android, skip these.

<ProductCard index={4} {...frontmatter.products[4]} />

Sennheiser's party trick is battery life that makes charging a monthly chore. Around 60 hours with noise cancelling on means two full work weeks between charges. The sound is the most balanced here, with real detail in the mids where voices and acoustic instruments live.

The noise cancelling is a clear step behind Sony and Bose on voices. The design is anonymous. For a home office where the problem is a dishwasher rather than a colleague, that trade makes sense.

## For calls on the move

This guide is about focus. If the job is being heard clearly on a windy platform, our [earbuds for work calls](/best-wireless-earbuds-for-work-calls) guide tested microphones first.

## Over-ear or earbuds?

Over-ears cancel more, last longer per charge, and are more comfortable for marathon sessions. Earbuds disappear into a pocket, work for a run at lunch, and do not flatten your hair before a client call. Most of us own both and reach for the over-ears on a focus day.

## The short version

- Best at making noise go away: Sony WH-1000XM6.
- Headphones give you headaches: Bose QC Ultra 2nd gen.
- Apple ecosystem, earbuds: AirPods Pro 3.
- Apple ecosystem, over-ears: AirPods Max USB-C.
- Charging is a chore: Sennheiser Momentum 4.

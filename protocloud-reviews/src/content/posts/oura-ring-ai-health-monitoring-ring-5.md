## What is an Oura ring, in plain language?

An **Oura ring** is a smart ring from Oura Health. No screen. No weekly charge anxiety like some watches. You pick a size, wear the **Oura ring** on your finger, and live with it.

The **Oura ring** sensors sit on the palm side of your finger, where blood flow is easier to read. While you sleep, the **Oura ring** watches heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing patterns, and blood oxygen on supported models. During the day it notices movement, stillness, and small temperature shifts that sometimes show up before you admit you are getting sick.

Most people know the **Oura ring** for two words: **sleep** and **readiness**. Sleep tells you how last night went. Readiness is the gentle nudge about whether today is a grind day or a take the long walk day. That combo made the **Oura ring** popular with athletes, tired parents, and founders who treat recovery like a KPI.

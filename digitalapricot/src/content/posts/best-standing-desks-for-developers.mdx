A standing desk is the one piece of furniture most developers use for eight hours a day, and the spec sheets are harder to compare than they should be. Height ranges, load ratings and warranties are all stated differently. This guide puts four widely sold desks side by side using the makers' own figures.

## How we picked

These picks come from manufacturers' published specifications, not our own hands-on testing. Each card links the page behind its specs, which we read in November 2026. Where a maker's figures disagreed with each other, we say so. Prices and discounts change often, so treat them as a snapshot.

## What to check before you buy

**Height range.** The lowest setting matters as much as the highest. If your elbows should sit at 90 degrees at the keyboard, shorter users need a low minimum and tall users need a high maximum. Compare the range with your own seated and standing heights, not with the average.

**Load capacity.** Add up the monitors, arms, PC and anything else on the desk. Makers quote dynamic load (what the motors lift) and static load (what the frame holds still). The dynamic figure is the one to plan around.

**Motors and stability.** Three of these are described by their makers as dual-motor; none of the makers publish a wobble figure, so stability is something to check in owner reviews.

**Warranty.** Frame, motors and electronics are covered for longer than the desktop in almost every case. Read which parts are covered for how long.

## Our picks

<ProductCard index={0} {...frontmatter.products[0]} />

The Uplift V3 is the one to start from. It has the widest set of sizes of the four, from 42 to 80 inches wide, a minimum height of 22.6 inches that works for shorter users, and a 15-year warranty that Uplift says covers the desktop as well as the frame, motors and electronics. The base price is $599 on Uplift's page, but desktop materials and larger sizes add to it, so budget for the configuration you actually want.

<ProductCard index={1} {...frontmatter.products[1]} />

The E7 Pro reaches 50.6 inches, which makes it one to check if you are tall. The Jarvis 3-stage goes slightly higher. It is dual-motor, has four memory presets, and FlexiSpot rates it at 396 lb dynamic load. FlexiSpot's own pages disagree on the static figure, quoting 573 lb in one place and 440 lb in another, so use the dynamic number. The frame is covered for 15 years, but the desktop only for 2 to 5 depending on material.

The Jarvis is the best documented desk here. Its page also says it uses dual-motor technology. It is the only one with a published lift speed, 1.5 inches per second, and it comes in three frame heights, including a low one that goes down to 23 inches. It is also the most expensive, and its 7-year frame warranty is shorter than the Uplift's and FlexiSpot's. Fully's desks are now sold through Herman Miller's store, and listings and warranty wording differ between regions, so check the page for your country.

<ProductCard index={3} {...frontmatter.products[3]} />

The Idåsen is the one you can see in a shop before you commit. Its height range of 24¾ to 50 inches matches the specialists, and IKEA offers a 10-year limited warranty. The catch is load: 154 lb is a fraction of the others, which is fine for one laptop and a monitor and not for a heavy multi-monitor rig. IKEA's page mentions a phone app, but does not list memory presets, so check that in store if it matters to you.

## What we could not confirm

Lift speed and noise are missing for most of these desks, and a stable top at full height is something only hands-on testing can show. We do not claim any of the four is quieter or steadier than another. Read owner reviews on that point before you decide.

## The short version

- One desk for most people: Uplift V3.
- Tall, or a heavy monitor setup: FlexiSpot E7 Pro.
- A desk you will use daily for years and want well documented: Fully Jarvis.
- Want to see it first, light setup: IKEA Idåsen.
- Tight budget in Belgium: the VASAGLE or the basic FlexiSpot.
- Wide desktop for two monitors on a budget: the 180 x 80 cm FlexiSpot.
